# Related projects and where ANTENA fits

*Checked with a limited web search on 13 November 2026. Facts change quickly: re-check before announcing or depending on anything below.*

- **NEC-2** (Fortran, LLNL, public) and **nec2++** (C++): <https://nec2.org/>, <https://www.physics.otago.ac.nz/reports/electronics/ETR2014-3.pdf>.
- **Arcanum** (Open Research Institute, Rust + Python, announced April 2026, design/documentation phase when checked) — a *new* method (Conformal Method of Moments with Exact Kernel) that also reads NEC files: <https://github.com/openresearchinstitute/arcanum>. ANTENA overlaps in audience and input format; **talk to them first** (issue #10) and aim for shared test models.
- **Gap targeted:** a classic NEC-2-equivalent solver in memory-safe Rust with a browser front end, good for teaching and as a cross-check for newer solvers.
